What GLB does and does not carry

GLB can carry meshes, skinning bones, and animation clips. Blender IK, constraints, drivers, and Rigify controls are not recreated here. Use Blender for full rigging and authoring.

Your reference images and GLB are read in this page only. Nothing is uploaded or saved to GitHub. The optional demo model and Three.js library are fetched from their named public sources.
